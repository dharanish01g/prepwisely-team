The idea

Team work shouldn't live in chat threads.

Small teams usually hand out work in WhatsApp groups, calls and sticky notes. Tasks get buried, nobody is sure who owns what, and personal to-dos sit in yet another app. PrepWisely Team gives every team one calm place for both.

Without it

  • "Who was doing this?" asked in a group chat
  • Deadlines scrolled away under new messages
  • Your own to-dos in a separate notes app
  • Work from different companies mixed together

With PrepWisely Team

  • Every shared task has a clear owner and a status
  • Due dates and priorities right on the task
  • Personal to-dos next to team work, kept private to you
  • One separate workspace for each team you belong to
How it works

Up and running in a minute.

No setup calls, no admin console. One person creates the team and everyone else joins with a code.

1

Create a team

Sign up with your email, name your team and pick a colour. You're the owner.

2

Share the invite code

Every team gets a six-letter code. Send it however you like, and teammates join in seconds.

K7PQ4M
3

Assign and track

Add tasks, assign them to anyone in the team and watch them move from To do to In progress to Done.

Features

Everything a small team needs, nothing it doesn't.

Personal to-dos

Private tasks just for you, kept beside your team work so your whole day is in one list.

Shared team tasks

Common tasks the whole team can see, assign to anyone, or leave open for whoever picks them up.

Separate workspaces

In more than one team or company? Each team is fully separate, and you switch between them with one tap.

Live updates

When a teammate adds, assigns or finishes a task, it shows up on your phone straight away. No refreshing.

Due dates and priorities

Mark what's urgent, set deadlines and see at a glance what's due today and what's overdue.

Team board

See all of the team's work by status, from To do through In progress to Done, and spot what's stuck.

Who it's for

Built for teams that move fast.

Anyone who has outgrown a group chat but doesn't need a heavy project-management tool.

Startups Agencies and studios Freelancers working with several clients Small business staff Student and project groups Volunteer and event teams

Your data stays yours

No ads, no tracking and no selling of data. Only members of a team can see what's inside it, and you can delete your account at any time from inside the app.
